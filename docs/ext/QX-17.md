# QX-17: the coin as a layer

Status: Draft. Written and first implemented 26 October 2026 in the reference tracker (`packages/tracker/src/coin.ts`), the mirror and the Quilt client, with redemption off; transfers are on from the home's genesis by the founder's decision of 27 October 2026. [EXTENSIONS.md](../EXTENSIONS.md) says what an extension may do; [COIN.md](../COIN.md) says what the coin is for and what it is not; QX-15 gives the stake it is locked as, QX-16 the verified square it is minted for, QX-11 and QX-12 the chain and the anchor its log has. A core client ignores all of it and computes the same map it did before.

**Abstract.** A tracker may carry a coin as a layer over its ledger, as QX-1 carries standing. The coin's own events live on one log of their own, the coin log, chained and anchored like a problem's; the squares that earn it and the strikes that slash it are read from the problem logs the same tracker holds. A fixed amount is minted each epoch and divided among the squares verified in it; what is minted vests with time; a root locks coin at a tracker's door to back a claim and loses it when the claim's square is struck; a payer escrows coin a problem's workers are paid from; a root burns coin for standing. Every balance is a function of the receipts and the published parameters, computed by the tracker, by the mirror and by anyone with the receipts; the tracker orders the lines and decides nothing. One tracker is the coin's home, and another that honours the coin reads the home's ledger at its door. Nobody needs a wallet, a gas token or a node to earn, hold, lock, pay or be paid.

## 1. The parameters

A tracker that carries the coin publishes its parameters in its well-known document under `coin`, and they are the coin's constitution: a reader computes balances from them, the mirror checks balances against them, and a change to any of them is a new version of the layer every reader can see.

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `extension` | `QX-17` |
| `home` | `here`, or the address of the tracker whose ledger this one reads |
| `genesis` | The time epochs count from |
| `epoch_h` | The length of an epoch in hours |
| `emission` | `{ per_epoch, decline_bp, period_epochs }`: the units minted in an epoch of the first period, and the basis points by which the amount falls after every `period_epochs` epochs |
| `vesting_h` | The hours over which a minting vests |
| `infra_bp` | The basis points of each epoch's minting that go to the treasury |
| `treasury` | The root that receives them |
| `unit` | How many units make a coin; every amount in the layer is in units, whole |
| `weights` | `{ cross_vendor, same_vendor, attest }`: a verified square's weight in its epoch, by how independent the set that made it was |
| `burn_rate` | The units of coin a point of standing costs at this tracker's door |
| `transfers_from`, `redeem_from` | The times from which `transfer` and `redeem` are kept, or `null` for never yet. The home tracker names its genesis as `transfers_from` (the founder's decision of 27 October 2026); `redeem_from` is null in this version |

## 2. The coin log

A pseudo-problem, `quilt:coin`, hashed as the identity log is, chained under QX-11 from its hash and anchored under QX-12 like any head. The tracker takes its events at `POST /coin/events` and serves them at `GET /coin/events?since=<seq>`. An event on it names no `problem` or `piece` in the event's own fields, as the identity log's do not; what it is about is in its body. An event's id is its identifier everywhere below.

| Type | Body | Valid when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lock` | `{ problem, piece, amount }` | `problem` is one this tracker holds and `piece` is one of its pieces; `amount` is a whole number of at least 1; the root's **free** coin at the receipt covers `amount`. The lock backs a stake of `floor(amount / burn_rate)` standing (section 5). |
| `pay` | `{ problem, amount, per_square }` | `problem` is one this tracker holds; `amount` and `per_square` are whole numbers of at least 1; the root's **free vested** coin at the receipt covers `amount`, which is escrowed to the problem (section 4). |
| `burn` | `{ amount }` | A whole number of at least 1; the root's free coin at the receipt covers it. The coin is gone, and the tracker credits `floor(amount / burn_rate)` standing (QX-15). |
| `transfer` | `{ to, amount }` | Received at or after `transfers_from`; `to` is an address; the root's free vested coin covers `amount`. |
| `redeem` | `{ amount, to? }` | Received at or after `redeem_from`; the door out ([COIN.md](../COIN.md)), not in this version. |

A delegate signs any of them only with it in `scope`. An event that fails a rule is dropped silently, as the core drops, and the reference tracker refuses it at the door with the reason.

## 3. What the layer derives from the problem logs

From the problem logs the tracker holds, as QX-1, QX-15 and QX-16 fold them, the layer derives:

| Line | From | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `verified` (problem, piece, root, weight, at) | a QX-16 match, at the reveal's receipt, one per root of the accepting set; the weight is `cross_vendor` where the set's reveals declare different vendors, `same_vendor` where they do not, `attest` for an attest set | emission |
| `forfeit` and `restore` (coin lock, at) | the QX-15 lock a coin lock is bound to, forfeited or restored | slashing |
| `release` (coin lock, at) | the QX-15 lock released, or the coin lock left unbound past the piece's lease | freeing a lock |
| `accepted` (problem, piece, roots, at) | each accepting set as the layer's fold stands, at its completion, with the roots `creditedRoots` gives: the reveals, not the runs | payments |
| `complete` (problem, at) | the problem complete, at the latest window close among its pieces | refunds |
| `mint` (epoch, at) | the end of every epoch at or before now | emission |

The coin log's lines and the derived lines are ordered together by time, derived lines before posted ones at the same instant and ties by id, which is deterministic from the receipts: anyone holding them computes the same balances, and the mirror does.

## 4. The rules

- **Emission.** Epoch `n` covers the hours from `genesis + n × epoch_h`. Its amount is `per_epoch`, reduced `floor(n / period_epochs)` times by `decline_bp` basis points, each reduction floored. At the epoch's end, `floor(amount × infra_bp / 10000)` goes to the treasury and the rest is divided among the epoch's `verified` lines pro rata by weight, each share floored; the remainder is unminted. An epoch with no verified line mints nothing, to nobody, and nothing is carried.
- **Vesting.** A root's vested total at a time is the sum over its mintings of each amount scaled by how much of `vesting_h` has passed since it, floored, plus everything it received by payment or transfer. Coin spent by a `pay`, a `transfer` or a `redeem` is drawn from the vested part; coin taken by a forfeit or a `burn` is drawn from the unvested part first. A root's **vested** coin is its vested total less what was drawn from it, bounded by its balance; its **unvested** coin is the rest. Coin taken from the unvested part does not slow the vesting of what remains: this is a coarsening, named in section 8.
- **Balance, free, free vested.** A root's **balance** is what it was minted and received less what it spent, burned, forfeited, transferred and redeemed. Locked and escrowed coin is part of the balance, set aside. **Free** is the balance less locked less escrowed; **free vested** is free or vested, whichever is less.
- **Locks.** A coin lock is live from its receipt. It is **bound** when a QX-15 lock of kind `coin` names it, and then ends as that lock ends: released with it, forfeited with it, restored with it. Left unbound, it is released at its receipt plus the piece's lease. A forfeit removes the lock's coin and as much again from the root's unvested coin as there is; a restore returns both.
- **Payments.** A `pay` escrows its amount to the problem. Each `accepted` on that problem after it pays `per_square` from the earliest escrow with coin left, divided equally among the set's credited roots, each share floored and the remainder staying in the escrow; a `complete` refunds every escrow's remainder to its payer. Received coin is vested.
- **Burn.** Removes the coin, from the unvested part first, and the tracker credits `floor(amount / burn_rate)` standing to the root from the receipt.
- **Transfers and redemption** are kept only from the times the parameters name; the home names its genesis for transfers and no time for redemption.
- **Arithmetic** is in whole units, and nothing rounds in anyone's favour: a remainder stays where it was.

## 5. Stake at the door, with coin

Under QX-15 a `lock { kind: "coin", amount, id }` on a problem's log names a coin lock by `id`, with `amount` the stake in standing. The tracker admits it when the coin lock is the root's, names this problem and piece, is live and unbound, and backs at least `amount`: `floor(units / burn_rate) ≥ amount`. At its home the tracker reads its own coin layer; elsewhere it reads `GET <home>/coin/lock/<id>`, and in either case it writes what it read into the receipt's wrapper as the resolver's word: `stake: { kind: "coin", amount, id, units, home, head }`. The QX-15 layer folds a coin-kind lock on that word, as a reader takes `custody`; the coin layer binds the coin lock to it. A coin-kind lock counts in no root's locked standing; a tracker that carries no coin and reads no home refuses the kind.

## 6. The reference tracker

- Carries the coin when its options name the parameters, as `QUILT_COIN` names them for the binary ([DEPLOY.md](../DEPLOY.md)); otherwise it has no coin log and refuses a coin-kind lock with `this tracker honours no coin lock`.
- Refuses at the door, with the reason: `the coin log takes lock, pay, burn, transfer and redeem`, `a coin event names no problem in its fields`, `no such problem here`, `names a piece this problem does not have`, `free coin M is below the lock of N`, `free vested coin M is below the pay of N`, `free coin M is below the burn of N`, `transfers are off`, `redemption is off`; and for a coin-kind QX-15 lock: `no such lock on the coin log`, `the lock is another root's`, `the lock names another piece`, `the lock is bound`, `the lock has ended`, `the lock holds U units, a stake of S at R a point`, `the home did not answer`.
- `GET /coin/lock/<id>`: the lock, its root, problem, piece, units, receipt, whether it is bound and to what, and its state: `live`, `released`, `forfeited`.
- `GET /coin/balance/<root>`: balance, vested, unvested, locked, escrowed, free, free vested, minted, received, burned, forfeited, at the head.
- `GET /coin/epochs?since=<n>`: each ended epoch's number, bounds, amount, treasury share, verified weight and what was minted.
- `GET /coin/state`: the head and every balance, for the mirror.
- `GET /.well-known/quilt` says `coin: { ...the parameters of section 1 }` and, under QX-15's `stake`, `coin: "here"` or the home's address and `burn_rate`.

## 7. The mirror

The mirror pulls the coin log into `ledger/coin.jsonl`, verifies its chain from the coin log's hash and that it only grew, and anchors its head as `anchors/coin/<seq>` beside the problems'. It then computes the layer itself, from the problems, the identity log and the coin log it holds and the parameters the tracker publishes, and compares every balance with the tracker's `/coin/state`: where they agree it writes `coin/balances.json`; where they do not it writes nothing and reports the head as one it cannot reproduce. A balance the mirror holds is therefore one anyone can recompute from receipts anyone can hold, under a head stamped in Bitcoin.

## 8. What this version does not do

- **One home.** The coin is as decentralized as the tracker that orders its log; the mirror's copy and the anchor bound that, and the door out ([COIN.md](../COIN.md)) is the later answer.
- **Vesting is per root, not per lot.** Coin taken from the unvested part does not slow the vesting of what remains, which favours a slashed root a little; a reader that wants lots can compute them from the same receipts, and a later version may say so.
- **A payer's coin waits for completion.** An escrow on a problem that never completes is never refunded; a payer chooses what to pay for.
- **Redemption is off.** The parameters name no time for it; transfers are kept from `transfers_from`, which the home tracker sets to its genesis.

## 9. The client

`quilt_me` and `quilt me` show, from a tracker that carries the coin, the key's balance, vested, locked, escrowed and free coin. `quilt pay <problem> <amount> <per_square>` and the tool `quilt_pay` escrow coin to a problem through the home's coin log. Handed a staked piece whose stake the key's free standing cannot cover, `next` asks the tracker with `coin=1`, and where the tracker honours coin and the key's free coin covers the stake at the burn rate, it posts the coin lock to the home, then the QX-15 lock naming it, then the claim, in one step. `quilt balance` prints the balance in full.

## 10. Tests

`packages/tracker/test/coin.test.ts` holds the rules: the parameters published; an epoch's minting divided by verified weight with the treasury's share first and nothing minted in an empty epoch; vesting by time; a coin lock refused over free coin and admitted, bound by a QX-15 lock at the door with the receipt carrying the word, and the refusals of a lock that is another root's, bound, too small or unknown; a pay refused over free vested coin, escrowed, paid out per square to credited roots and the rest held; a burn refused over free coin and credited as standing; a forfeit taking the lock and as much again from the unvested, and a restore returning both; transfer and redeem refused while off; the balances at the head. `packages/mirror/test/coin.test.ts` holds the mirror: the coin log mirrored and anchored, the balances reproduced, and a head whose balances differ refused. `packages/client/test/coin.test.ts` holds the client: the text of `quilt_me`, a pay through the tool, and the tools' words describing and never instructing.
