# QX-24: answers by key, and the keys a reader leaves out

Status: Draft. Written and first implemented 16 November 2026 in the core, the reference tracker and the Quilt client; [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#the-shape-of-it) names the files that carry it out. [EXTENSIONS.md](../EXTENSIONS.md) says what an extension may do; [reading 2](../v2/READING.md) section 3 is the record this extension reads, every reading of a piece with how it counts; [QX-20](QX-20.md) is the record kind, which keeps readings side by side on a piece that asks for it, where this extension tells them side by side on every piece. A core client ignores all of it and computes the same map it did before.

**Abstract.** The record keeps every reading under the key that signed it, and until this extension a reader was shown the ones a rule had picked: the two of an accepted set, the readings of a record piece, the two sides of a dispute. A reading alone on its piece was on the record and in no listing, and a reader who came to distrust a key had no way to read a problem without it. This extension tells a problem as what it is underneath: the answers given, each with every key that gave it. Nothing is picked and nothing is ranked. And it gives the reader one choice: a list of keys to leave out, kept by the reader and told to nobody, over which the answers are told again. It adds no event and moves nothing: no state, no set, no standing and no coin. The squares stay the tracker's own count, and what a reader makes of the answers is the reader's.

## 1. The answers of a piece

An answer is what the readings of a piece that agree on its agree fields say, with every reading that says it and the key each was signed by.

Taken exactly, for one piece:

- The **readings** are the record's, as reading 2 section 3 lists them: every reveal the line's record holds on the piece, in receipt order, each with how it counts, one of `counted`, `kept`, `aside` and `struck`, and why where it counts for nothing. A reading is told with the root it counts for, the key that signed it, the vendor and the model its reveal declares, the time of its receipt, and its return.
- Two readings **say the same answer** where their returns agree on the piece's agree fields as the core reads agreement ([CORE.md](../CORE.md) section 6): the fields the piece's `agree` names, or every required field where it names none. On a `check` or `score` piece, whose return is a patch, and on a piece with no schema, two readings say the same answer where their returns have one hash.
- A reading joins the first answer it agrees with, in receipt order. So the answers stand in the order the first reading of each was received, and the readings under an answer in receipt order. No answer is ranked above another: the order is the order of arrival and says nothing of which is right.
- An answer **says** its agree fields as its first reading gave them, and nothing where the piece returns a patch or has no schema.
- An answer counts its **roots** and its **vendors**: those of its readings that are `counted` or `kept`, each once. A reading that is `struck` or `aside` is listed with the rest and counted in neither, since a decision or the grounds already put it out, and its entry says which. A root counts once however many of its keys signed.

A piece with no reading has no answers and is not listed. Nothing here reads the piece's state, and the state does not read this: a piece is accepted, disputed or read once as reading 2 says, whatever its answers are.

## 2. The keys a reader leaves out

A reader may leave keys out. A reading is left out where its root or the key that signed it is one of them, and the answers are told again over the readings that remain.

- The list is addresses. An address is matched whatever capitals it is written in.
- Told again means section 1 over the remaining readings alone: the answers, their order, their roots and their vendors. An answer whose every reading is left out is not told, and a piece whose every reading is left out is not listed. The telling says how many readings it left out.
- The list is **the reader's own**. No event carries it, no tracker is told it, and a tracker's listing leaves no key out. A reader holds the tracker's listing and tells the answers again; the function is the core's `tally`, so a tracker, a client and a phone that hold the same readings and the same list tell the same answers.
- Leaving a key out moves nothing for anyone else. The key's readings stay on the record, the squares they made stand, and its standing and coin are what they were. A reader who believes a square is wrong brings a challenge ([QX-1](QX-1.md)), which is the record's own way to strike a set, and which this does not replace.

## 3. The reference tracker and the client

- `GET /p/<hash>/answers` gives `{ problem, now, pieces }`: each piece that has a reading, in the problem's file order, as `{ piece, name, verifier, state, readings, left_out, answers }`, with `left_out` 0, and each answer as `{ says, roots, vendors, readings }`, each reading `{ id, key, root, signer, vendor, model, at, counts, why, digest, return }`. `signer` is the name the root chose on the identity log, or null. `return` is null on a `check` or `score` piece. The route reads no query. `GET /.well-known/quilt` says `answers: { extension: "QX-24" }`.
- `quilt read` has an `Answers by key` section after the disputed, where a piece has a reading: each piece with its state, how many readings and answers, and each answer as the quoted agree fields, with the address, the name and the model of every key that gave it, the keys and vendors behind it, and why a reading counts for nothing where it does. A piece no other section shows the returns of, one read once among them, has each return in full under its answers. The reader's own readings are marked.
- `quilt_leave_out` leaves a key out of this home's reads or brings it back, with a reason kept beside it. The list is in the home's library and nothing is signed or posted. A read tells the answers without the keys left out and says which keys it left out and how many of their readings; `quilt_me` lists the keys with the reason given for each. A piece's line says how many of its readings were left out. The state beside each piece and the other sections of a read are the tracker's own count, which leaves no key out, and the read says so.

## 4. What this draft leaves open

- **An address costs nothing.** A key left out comes back as another, so leaving out is a tool against a careless key and a weak one against a determined one. The stronger list is the other way round: the keys a reader does count, by name, by standing, or by the product that witnessed them ([QX-18](QX-18.md)). The tally already counts roots and vendors behind each answer; a list of keys to count alone is not built.
- **Saying it on the record.** The list is private. A signed statement that a key leaves another out, with its reason, would let readers weigh each other's lists, and would be a new event with its own rules against abuse.
- **The map for this reader.** The squares stay the tracker's count. A fold of a problem without a root's events, which would say what the map is for a reader who leaves that root out, is the core's `read` over a shorter record and is not offered at a door, since each list would cost a fold.
- **What an answer earns.** Standing and coin follow accepted sets as before, and a reading alone on its piece earns nothing. Whether a reading should earn by what stands behind its answer is not decided here.
- **Everything a key said.** `GET /k/<address>/work` lists a key's latest readings. A reader weighing a key across every problem would want them all, by root.
- The card and the iPhone app do not show the list or the answers yet.

## 5. Tests

`packages/core/test/answers.test.ts` holds the rule: the readings that agree set beside each other in the order of arrival, the agree fields or every required field, a reading struck or set aside listed and not counted, a root counted once, a patch by its hash, a key left out by its root or by the key that signed, whatever capitals, and nothing told where every reading is out. `packages/tracker/test/answers.test.ts` holds the route: nothing listed of a problem nobody read, a reading alone on its piece with the key that signed it, a set of two with a third kept beside it under one answer, two that differ under two, a reader's telling without a key, and the route the same whatever query it is given. `packages/client/test/answers.test.ts` holds the words and the list: the section of `quilt read` whole, a key left out with nothing posted and the next read told without it, another home's read as it was, `quilt_me`'s line, the key brought back, and what is not an address refused.
