# QX-13: the announcement

Status: Draft. The event is from 26 October 2026 (APP.md, "The announce event"); this document was written 2 November 2026, when the body grew to carry the work on a problem, and the reference tracker and the Quilt client implement it. [EXTENSIONS.md](../EXTENSIONS.md) says what an extension may do; [QX-14](QX-14.md) carries the announce to relays. A core client ignores all of it.

**Abstract.** The core has no topics field and should not grow one, and an index needs something to index. An announcement is an event of its own, signed by the owner or by a seeder, kept by a tracker beside the problem's log and by relays on its own, and ignored by the reducer. It says what the problem is called and what it is about, and, since 2 November 2026, who owns it, what it forks, and how much work is on it and of what quality, as the announcer's tracker counted it when the announce was posted. So a problem found on a relay alone shows what a problem found on a tracker shows, as a dated snapshot.

## 1. The event

```json
{ "quilt": 1, "type": "announce", "problem": "sha256:…", "key": "0x…", "ts": "…",
  "body": { "title": "…", "slug": "…", "done_when": "…", "topics": ["citations", "ai-safety"],
            "owner": "0x…", "from": null, "pieces": 912, "counts": { "open": 180, "accepted": 640, "disputed": 3 },
            "work": { "returned": 700, "accepted": 640, "verified": 120, "disputed": 3, "traced": { "both": 400, "one": 150, "none": 60 }, "readers": 14, "vendors": 3, "last": "2026-11-02T18:40:00Z" },
            "trackers": ["https://…/"], "mirrors": ["https://…/"] },
  "sig": "0x…" }
```

- `title`, `slug` and `done_when` are the envelope's, word for word.
- `topics` are at most eight words of at most forty characters each, through the plain-text gate, and are the announcer's own.
- `owner` is the envelope's owner. An older announce without it is read as the signer's, which is right for an owner's and wrong for a seeder's, which is why it is here.
- `from` is the envelope's `from`, or null: the problem this one forks (CORE.md section 2), so a directory groups forks under the problem they fork without opening each.
- `pieces` is how many pieces the problem has, `counts` the pieces by state, and `work` the tracker's directory row (APP.md, Search): pieces returned, accepted, verified by the owner's gold and disputed, the accepted squares by their grade (QX-19 section 3), the distinct roots and vendors that read, and the latest reading's receipt time. All whole numbers, `last` a time or null. They are the announcer's word about a moment; a reader with a tracker's row takes the row.
- `trackers` and `mirrors` are where the problem can be opened, as hints a link carries.

## 2. The door

The reference tracker keeps one announce per problem and key, the latest replacing the earlier, and refuses one whose `title`, `slug` or `done_when` is not the envelope's (`announce: title, slug and done_when are the envelope's`), whose topics are not as above (`announce: at most eight short topics through the gate`), whose `owner` is present and not the envelope's (`announce: owner is the envelope's`), whose `from` is present and not the envelope's (`announce: from is the envelope's`), or whose `pieces`, `counts` or `work` are present and not of the shape above (`announce: pieces, counts and work are whole numbers`). It checks shape and the envelope's facts, nothing more: the numbers are the announcer's. Its index shows a problem's topics as the union over every announce it keeps.

## 3. Who announces, and when

- `publish` announces where the envelope went: `counts` as `{ open: n }` and `work` all zeros, since nothing has happened yet.
- The owner's turn (the part of `quilt away` that needs no agent, which the hosted on-ramp runs hourly for the keys it holds) announces a problem again when the work on it has moved since the last announce the home recorded, with the tracker's row of the moment: the same topics, the same signer. It announces too where a relay this home is configured with has not been offered the announce yet, so a relay added later gets every owned problem at the next turn, once; one that was down then gets the next announce the work earns. Hourly at most, the owner only. A seeder may announce too, with its own topics; a reader prefers the owner's.
- A relay replaces an announce by its signer and problem (QX-14), so an owner's latest stands there as it stands on the tracker.

## 4. What this does not do

It does not make the numbers true. A reader who opens the problem gets the tracker's row and the chain, and the announce was only how they found it. It does not rank problems: the directory sorts by the work it is shown, and the person chooses.

## 5. Tests

`packages/tracker/test/directory.test.ts` holds the door: an announce with a wrong `owner` or a malformed `work` refused, a well-formed one kept, the topics in the index. `packages/client/test/relay.test.ts` holds the client: the publish body with its owner, counts and zero work; the owner's turn announcing again once a square is accepted and not again while nothing moved; and the reading of an announce on a relay, the owner's before a seeder's.
